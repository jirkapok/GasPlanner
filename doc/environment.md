# Environment

* `Salinity`: Because salinity of water changes its density, it also affects the pressure of the water column at depth. As a result, it affects the tissues loading. See also wiki for [brine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brine). E.g., 22 meters (73 ft) corresponds to 3.222 bar (46.7 psi) in salt water. You can choose from fresh water, salt (sea) water and Brackish (EN13319) water. Brackish water is somewhere between salt and fresh. It is used by some computers as the only option (Suunto). In case your dive computer doesn't contain Brackish water use salt water (value with higher salt content).

| Salinity | Location | Salinity [gram/liter] | Density [kg/m3] |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Salt | Red sea, Mediterranean sea | 38 | 1028 |
| Brackish (EN13319) | Black sea, Baltic sea | 28 | 1020 |
| Fresh | Lakes | 0.1 | 1000 |

* `Altitude`: Similar to salinity, altitude has an effect on decompression. The higher you are, the lower the atmospheric pressure around you. 0 m.a.s.l. means sea level, e.g., 1.0132 bar. Diving at higher altitude results in greater decompression requirements (e.g., shortened no decompression limits).

| Altitude [m.a.s.l.] | Imperial [f.a.s.l.] | Air pressure [bar] |
| ---  | ---  | ---  |
| 0    | 0    | 1.01 |
| 400  | 1312 | 0.97 |
| 700  | 2297 | 0.93 |
| 2000 | 6562 | 0.79 |
