# Weight

The weight calculator helps to estimate the required additional lead the diver needs to use to compensate for weight lost during the dive as gas from the provided tank is consumed. This does not account for protection suit weight compensation using additional lead. E.g., if you consume 150 b (2175 psi) from 15 L (125 cuft) tank, you will need to add 2.8 kg (6.1 lb) of lead to be neutrally buoyant. This calculator is based on the assumption that 1 liter of dry air at 15 °C weighs 1,225 g.
